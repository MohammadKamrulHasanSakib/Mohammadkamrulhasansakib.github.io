About Me

Mohammad Kamrul Hasan Sakib is a Marketing and Merchandising Executive at Renaissance Group, managing core knitwear categories across Men’s, Women’s, and Kids’ lines (including Jersey, Sportswear, Heavyweight, The Set, Self, and NTN Brands) for key UK accounts such as Next Retail Ltd.

He leads front-line marketing engagement and direct buyer communication—translating client desires into feasible product offerings, handling strategic costing, managing Time & Action (T&A) calendars, and executing order confirmations. Proficient in ERP logic, Excel, and marketing presentations, Sakib ensures smooth production handovers, deadline compliance, and proactive problem-solving.

Parallel to his corporate career, Sakib has a proven track record in startup building and venture strategy. Between 2020 and 2024, he co-founded Morning Agro, scaling the venture from an initial bootstrap capital of 17,000 BDT to over 50 Lakh BDT in raised funding and generating 45 Lakh BDT in monthly revenue before transitioning out. Previously, he founded Tech Karigor School, securing 15 Lakh BDT in seed investment to manage operations over a two-year run.

An active leader in strategic innovation challenges, Sakib led his team to the Grand Final of the Mujib 100 Idea Contest, pitching a technology-driven business solution tailored to Bangladesh’s transport and tourism sector.

He holds a B.Sc. in Textile Engineering from Rangpur Textile Engineering College (affiliated with BUTEX) and is currently pursuing a Post Graduate Diploma (PGD) in Textile Industry Management at the Bangladesh University of Textiles (BUTEX). His career vision is to become a textile industry leader who builds sustainable, long-term partnerships within the global apparel business.
